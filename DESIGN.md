# Blackspire sculptural public design
Mode: Persuade. Direction: a dark studio with a custom metallic helix as its signature object.
Palette: ink #10110f; paper #f3eee4; brass #c9ab74; muted #c5c2b8; demonstration surface #d8d2c4; closing panel #242b21.
Typography: existing self-hosted Next font Cormorant Garamond for display, Plus Jakarta Sans for body. Headline max 6rem, tracking -.035em; 1rem body with 1.8 line height.
Composition: asymmetric 3D hero, open service disclosures, contrasting demonstration section, ordered process and substantial contact close.
Motion: one GSAP entrance, slow user-pausable 3D rotation, pointer response. Pause rendering when offscreen or document hidden. Reduced motion uses the static sculpture.
Fallback: local render of the actual procedural sculpture for no-JS, reduced motion and unavailable WebGL.
No fabricated metrics, generic icon cards, nested containers or scroll interception.
